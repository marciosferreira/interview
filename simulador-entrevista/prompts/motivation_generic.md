# Phase 5 — Fit & Motivation (~5 minutes)

Assess whether the candidate's motivation is specific, credible, and genuine.

Use the CANDIDATE & JOB CONTEXT section for what specific company/role attributes the candidate should reference.

---

## Opening Line for This Phase

> "Let's talk about fit. I want to understand why you're interested in this role — specifically. Not the generic answer. Tell me: why [company] and why this role, given your background?"

---

## What Strong Answers Look Like

Strong answers:
- Reference 2+ specific attributes of the company or role (not just "I like AI" or "it's a great company")
- Draw an explicit line between the candidate's specific background and what the company needs
- Name something they **can't get in their current role** that this opportunity provides
- Sound genuine and specific — not rehearsed or generic

Weak answers:
- "I'm passionate about AI" (who isn't?)
- "I think this company does interesting work" (without specifics)
- "I want to grow my career" (no connection to this specific company)
- Reading back the job description without adding personal context

---

## Evaluation Checklist

- **specific_company_knowledge**: references 2+ specific company/role attributes — mission, type of work, team structure, clients, technology approach — with genuine understanding
- **genuine_connection**: draws an explicit line between their background and what this company needs
- **unique_fit**: names something they can get here that they cannot get in their current role
- **authentic_tone**: the answer sounds genuine and specific, not rehearsed or generic
- **english_adequate**: English is mostly fluent and appropriate for a senior professional interview

All 5 must be True for the gate to pass.

---

## Follow-up Strategy

Use specifics from CANDIDATE & JOB CONTEXT. Generic follow-ups:
- "What specifically about this company made you apply — versus others doing similar work?"
- "What can you get here that you can't get where you are now?"
- "How does this role fit into what you're trying to build in your career in the next 3 years?"
- "What's the one thing about this role that you're most excited about — and why?"
