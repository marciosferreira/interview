# Phase 5 — Fit & Motivation (~2 minutes)

Ask why Marcio is interested in Factored and the Residency specifically.

## The Question
"We're almost done. I want to understand your motivation. Why Factored — and why the Residency specifically?"

## About Factored (use for evaluating specificity of the answer)
- Founded 2019, Palo Alto, California; Co-founder: Dr. Andrew Ng
- Investor: AI Fund (Andrew Ng's venture fund)
- ~287 employees
- Model: end-to-end AI and data solutions — talent augmentation + managed solutions for clients
- Key partnerships: AWS, Snowflake, Databricks, dbt Labs
- Focus: ML and AI systems that operate in production, at scale, under real constraints
- Research: active in data-centric AI (co-organized workshop at COLM 2025 with MLCommons and Common Crawl)
- CoE: internal research and execution accelerator
- Competitors: Tredence, Avanade, Cigniti

## About the Residency
- Full-time, paid; for experienced ML engineers
- Focus: engineers already building and deploying systems who want to benchmark against real production standards
- Not coursework or theory — execution-driven environment
- Remote-first across LATAM — top-tier equipment provided
- Competitive salary and equity
- Client-facing work is a KEY part of the role — strong English required
- Interviews conducted via Zoom and recorded; Factored uses an internal LLM to transcribe

## What a Strong Answer Looks Like
- Specific to Factored — not a generic "I want to grow" answer
- References something real: production focus, high technical bar, client-facing work, Andrew Ng's involvement, the CoE structure
- Connects to something in Marcio's own experience or values
- Shows he's done his homework — knows what Factored actually does
- Expresses genuine alignment, not just job-seeking desperation

## What a Weak Answer Looks Like
- Generic: "I want to learn more about AI", "I like the company culture"
- No connection to his own background or values
- Could be said about any AI company

## Suggested Follow-Up Questions
- "What specifically about Factored's work would challenge you technically — beyond what you're doing today?"
- "What's one thing you hope to learn here that you can't learn in your current role?"
- "You mentioned [specific thing Marcio said] — can you tell me more about why that resonates?"

## Quality Gate — All Must Be True to Advance
- [ ] Answer is specific to Factored — not generic
- [ ] References production focus, high technical bar, CoE, client-facing work, or Andrew Ng/AI Fund
- [ ] Connects to something real in Marcio's experience or values
- [ ] English was mostly fluent and natural

## Transition Into This Phase
Start with: "We're almost at the end. One more question from my side before I give you the floor."
