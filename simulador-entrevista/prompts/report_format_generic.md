# Phase Report — Structured Feedback Format

You have the full transcript of the interview phase just completed, including all of the candidate's answers and follow-up responses.

Generate a structured feedback report using EXACTLY this format. Be specific — reference actual things the candidate said (or failed to say).

---

## Report Format

```
============================================
📋 PHASE REPORT
============================================

CONTENT FEEDBACK

Strength:
[Quote a specific phrase or moment from the candidate's answer that worked well. Explain why it was effective.]

Gap:
[Name the specific checklist item(s) that were not met. Be direct and specific.]

Tip:
[One concrete, actionable suggestion. A reframe, a missing element, or a phrase they could use.]

MISSED OPPORTUNITIES

[2–3 specific things the candidate had the background to mention but didn't.
Start each with "You could have mentioned…"
Reference actual content from their resume (see CANDIDATE & JOB CONTEXT).]

VOCABULARY & FRAMING TO PRACTICE

[3–5 terms or phrases relevant to this phase and role.
Format each as: "[term/phrase] → [why it matters for this role]"]

LANGUAGE FEEDBACK

Grammar: [specific errors found, or "No major errors this phase"]
Word choice: [weak, informal, or awkward words — suggest better alternatives]
Sentence structure: [flag run-ons, fragments, or convoluted phrasing]
Register: [appropriate for a senior technical interview with a global client?]
Expressions to improve: [phrases that sound non-native or translated — with natural alternatives]
Fluency rating: [Fluent / Mostly Fluent / Needs Work]

SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENT

[Rewritten version of the weakest answer or the weakest part of an answer.
2–4 sentences max. Show the better version — don't describe it.]

PHASE SUMMARY

Checklist status:
[List each checklist item with ✅ (passed) or ❌ (not met)]

Overall: [Strong / Adequate / Weak — one sentence summary]
============================================
```

---

## Rules

- Be specific. Do not write generic feedback that could apply to any candidate.
- Reference actual things the candidate said (or failed to say).
- The MISSED OPPORTUNITIES section is the most valuable coaching output. Make it concrete and candidate-specific.
- The SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENT must rewrite — not describe. Show the better version.
- Never inflate. If the phase was weak, say so clearly and constructively.
