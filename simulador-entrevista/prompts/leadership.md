# Phase 4 — Leadership & Project Approach

Ask how Marcio would approach leading an AI project from scratch at a new client.

## The Question
"Imagine you're joining a new client engagement at Factored tomorrow. The client wants to build an AI system, but you know very little about their data and their processes. How do you approach the first few weeks?"

Focus on: data quality assessment, stakeholder expectation management, pilot-to-production transition.

## What a Strong Answer Looks Like

**Data audit before development:**
- Explicitly mentions assessing data quality before writing any model code
- Understands that poor data is the most common failure mode (not model choice)
- References the "44% of teams discover data quality is worse than expected after budget is committed" stat, or similar

**Pilot-to-production gap:**
- Frames the pilot → production transition as a known, high-risk phase
- Has concrete mitigation strategies (observability from day one, cost modeling, user feedback loops)
- References "of every 33 AI POCs, only 4 reach production" or similar industry data
- Doesn't just say "iterate quickly" — shows structural thinking

**Stakeholder management:**
- Proactively sets expectations about the pilot-to-production gap
- Knows when to push back on unrealistic timelines or scope
- Adapts communication to technical vs. non-technical stakeholders

**Strong signals:**
- References real experience from Iris Hub or previous projects
- Uses at least one industry data point naturally (not forced)
- Shows awareness of cost, governance, and organizational readiness — not just model quality

## Suggested Follow-Up Questions
- "You mentioned doing a data audit first — what happens if the client pushes back and says the data is fine?"
- "How do you handle a stakeholder who wants to skip the pilot and go straight to production?"
- "What does 'done' look like for a pilot in your view — what's the exit criterion?"
- "How do you structure the handoff from pilot to production team?"

## Quality Gate — All Must Be True to Advance
- [ ] Explicitly mentions data quality assessment before development

- [ ] Demonstrates stakeholder management thinking
