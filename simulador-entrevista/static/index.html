The interview is where the role is won or lost

Whether it's your first conversation or the final round, what you say — and how you say it — decides the outcome. Behavioral questions, role-specific scenarios, fit and motivation: this tool prepares you for all of them, tailored to your background and the specific job you're after.

The 6 phases

6 phases. One complete interview.

Works for any field — tech, finance, consulting, marketing, operations, and more. The AI starts from the role and adapts every question to the details you choose to add.

PHASE 1
Elevator Pitch
Introduce yourself in 90 seconds. Get instant feedback on clarity, structure, and how well you frame your background for this specific role.
PHASE 2
CAR Story
Tell a structured story — Challenge, Action, Result — about a real project. Works for any industry: a campaign, a deal, a migration, a product launch.
PHASE 3
Role-Specific Questions
3 targeted questions generated from the role and any details you add. Engineer, analyst, manager, or consultant: each interview is different.
PHASE 4
Leadership & Approach
How you lead, handle ambiguity, and drive work to completion — assessed against what the role actually requires.
PHASE 5
Fit & Motivation
Why this role, why this company, why now — coached until your answer sounds genuine, not rehearsed.
PHASE 6
Your Questions
Practice asking smart, well-researched questions. The questions you ask signal as much as the answers you give.
