Why this round is the one that matters most
In most hiring processes — tech, finance, consulting, marketing — the behavioral interview is the first filter. Only candidates who clear it move on to the next rounds. Failing here means your skills never even get evaluated. This tool prepares you for exactly that round — so you arrive knowing what to say, how to say it, and how you'll be judged.
How it works
6 phases. One complete interview.
Works for any field — tech, finance, consulting, marketing, operations, and more. The AI adapts every question to your resume and the actual job description.
PHASE 1
🎤
Elevator Pitch
Introduce yourself in 90 seconds. Get instant feedback on clarity, structure, and how well you frame your background for this specific role.
PHASE 2
🚗
CAR Story
Tell a structured story — Challenge, Action, Result — about a real project. Works for any industry: a campaign, a deal, a migration, a product launch.
PHASE 3
🔍
Role-Specific Questions
3 targeted questions generated from the actual job description — not generic. Engineer, analyst, manager, or consultant: each interview is different.
PHASE 4
🧭
Leadership & Approach
How you lead, handle ambiguity, and drive work to completion — assessed against what the role actually requires.
PHASE 5
💡
Fit & Motivation
Why this role, why this company, why now — coached until your answer sounds genuine, not rehearsed.
PHASE 6
🙋
Your Questions
Practice asking smart, well-researched questions. The questions you ask signal as much as the answers you give.
